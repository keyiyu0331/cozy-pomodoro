## Audio — `public/assets/audio/`

Field recordings from Freesound, all released as Creative Commons 0 (public
domain). Each was trimmed, looped, levelled and compressed by
`scripts/process-audio.mjs`.

| File | Recording | Licence |
|---|---|---|

| `storm.m4a` | "230405 Rain heavy, concrete, trees, M10 ParaEM272s, Toronto 7am" by TRP — https://freesound.org/people/TRP/sounds/717843/ | CC0 |
| `thunder.m4a` | "Thunder.wav" by DiArchangeli — https://freesound.org/people/DiArchangeli/sounds/128307/ | CC0 |
| `wind.m4a` | "windy winter day, wind in trees, from distance.wav" by lwdickens — https://freesound.org/people/lwdickens/sounds/259968/ | CC0 |
| `birds.m4a` | "Early Summer Morning Ambience with Birds, Berlin" by Pfannkuchn — https://freesound.org/people/Pfannkuchn/sounds/457616/ | CC0 |

were matched to the downloaded files by their length and format; confirm them
against what was actually downloaded.

The end-of-phase chimes are synthesised live in the browser by `src/audio/chime.ts`
(no audio file).
